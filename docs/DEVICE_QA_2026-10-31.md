## Bugs found and fixed

1. **Arena did not cover tall screens** (2.22:1) — a grey band filled the bottom ~18 % of the screen, because the 1080×1920 arena art was not scaled for `expand`.
   Fixed in `scripts/battle/battle_controller.gd`: every world node is reparented into a `World` container, the design area is centred in the real viewport, and the background is scaled to *cover* (not contain). Verified on device after rebuild — screenshot `docs/preview/device_14_fixed.png`.

## Open visual issues (not blocking, art-related)

- The placeholder tree (600×680 solid rect) overlaps the pedestal ring; with real art (transparent canopy) this is expected to read correctly. Worth re-checking with the first real tree sprite.
- Nav labels and some rows sit close to the bottom safe area; fine on this device, still unverified on a notched device.
- Upgrade button uses the gold variation; spec does not mandate a colour, kept for attention.
