## Combat rules (spec 4.7)

- `DamageResolver`: `base * damage_multiplier * falloff` → crit → flat armor with
  a 10 % floor → `* target damage_taken_multiplier`. `is_true_damage` skips armor.
- Splash falloff is linear from 1.0 at impact to `splash_min_ratio` at the edge.
- Slow refreshes the timer and keeps the strongest percentage (no stacking).
- Floating damage labels: 25 pooled, 0.7 s rise+fade, crit larger and gold.

## Economy

- Starting cash **120** (`WaveTable.starting_cash`, spec §4.10 updated, ADR-020);
  kill cash as above.
- Wave clear `60 + 14*wave` (M3.1; ADR-004 formula slot, ADR-021 values); prep
  skip `min(10 + 2*wave, 120)`.
- Run rewards (M3 UI): coins `sum(8 + 2*wave)`, gems `floor(cleared/5)`, XP
  `10*cleared`; boss waves carry +50 coins in wave data for the reward bundle.
- Run cash is lost; coins, gems, XP and best wave persist.

| Track | Effect / level | Cost base | Growth | Cap | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Range | +3 % | 40 | 1.30 | — | 30 |
| Regen | +0.3 % max HP/s | 80 | 1.40 | — | 30 |

Cost of the next level = `CostCurve.cost_at(base, growth, level + 1)`
(`base * growth^level`, rounded). Effects are additive; crit chance clamps at
60 %. Expected tree DPS = `damage*(1+c)*(1+crit*(crit_mult-1)) / interval`, with
crit chance capped and crit damage added to the 2.0x multiplier. The DPS test
asserts +1 damage level = +8.0 % DPS.

Transaction rules (M3): every spend goes through `RunWallet.try_spend()` and
returns `SpendResult`; `RunWallet.add()` rejects amounts ≤ 0. Hold-to-buy repeats
at 8/s and stops on the first failed result, so it can never overspend or go
negative (unit test simulates 5 s). Sell refund stays 70 % of invested (verified).

## Balance gate (M3.1 — MET)

**Target (M3.1 brief):** median cleared waves 8–14 across ≥3 openings/seeds, no run
below 7, none above 16, no wave with >40 concurrent enemies, and a defeat still
reachable around wave 12.

**Method:** `tools/balance_probe.gd`, one run per (opening × seed), 8× simulation,
greedy buying and prep skips, 3 openings (`fill` = 8 archers then upgrades,
`four` = 4 archers → L4 → fill, `two` = 2 archers → L5 → fill) × 6 seeds = 18 runs.
The probe reports damage share by source and per-wave peak enemies, and resets
GameState/SaveService/RunState between runs.

| Opening | Cleared waves (seeds 1–6) | Median | Defeat on wave |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fill` (8 archers → upgrades) | 11, 11, 10, 10, 10, 10 | 10 | 11–12 |
| `four` (4 → L4 → fill) | 9, 9, 9, 9, 9, 9 | 9 | 10 |
| `two` (2 → L5 → fill) | 9, 9, 9, 9, 9, 9 | 9 | 10 |

Overall: **median 9, min 9, max 11**; peak concurrent enemies **11** (limit 40);
every run exits 0 (no crash) and ends in tree defeat. Damage share in the `fill`
runs is towers ~87 %, Tree ~11 %, units ~3 % (before the pass: towers ~50 %, Tree
~45 % and only 2–5 towers were affordable).

**Before the pass** (same probe, spec numbers): all 9 baseline runs cleared 4 waves
and died on wave 5 (towers 2–5, starting cash 50 could not buy the 60-cash archer).

**Changes that produced the result** (all data; ADR-020/021):
starting cash 50 → 120; tower ranges 300/280/260 → 400/370/340; HP/wave 1.16 →
1.11; counts 6+1.2w → 5+0.8w; boss group ×1.6 → ×1.20; spawn interval 0.72/0.35 →
1.00/0.45; clear bonus 20+5w → 60+14w; kill cash ×1.09 → ×1.12. Tower upgrade
cost growth stays at the spec's 1.55 and the spec's tree/unit/enemy base stats are
unchanged. `M3.md` still lists its original "wave 15+" line; M3.1's brief
supersedes it with the 8–14 distribution above.
